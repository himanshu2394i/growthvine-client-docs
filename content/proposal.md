# Proposal film

A private briefing that walks one client through their proposal, question by
question. Private to that client. First test: a two-option ₹3 Cr proposal (16 Nov 2026),
2:45, 14 scenes, rendered to MP4 with music.

## Default question chain (offer it, the advisor edits)

| # | Question in the client's voice | Answered with |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Where should it all go? (cold open) | The total counting up |
| 2 | Why not put it all in one good fund? | The total splitting into buckets, each with its job ("One fund does one job. Your money has four.") |
| 2b | So what's actually inside each part? | Every holding under its bucket with amounts, plus the mix by product type (MF, SIF, PMS, AIF, structured) |
| 3 | Markets have gone nowhere. Is this a bad time to start? | Market return over 1, 3 and 5 years; "not a forecast" |
| 4 | Are these funds actually any good? | Growth of ₹1 lakh in each fund against the market; which products publish no comparable returns |
| 4b | How far have they fallen in bad periods? | Worst fall of each fund, of the proposed mix and of the benchmark, on one data set and period; the rupee fall at the low on the client's amounts; what Stability holds, with duration translated into a price move |
| 5 | Am I too exposed to any one company? | The largest company in % and in rupees of the client's money (look-through), the next few companies, the largest sectors, and what the look-through covers |
| 6 | Do the funds hold the same stocks, and move together? | Holdings overlap (the highest pair in rupees per ₹100), monthly return correlation, and the mix's yearly swing against the weighted average of the funds' swings (an unflattering result is shown as it is) |
| 7 | What if I suddenly need money? | Money reachable within a week, a year, after lock-ins, with which products sit in each, and the share reachable in a week |
| 8 | Option 1 or Option 2? | Buckets side by side, plus holdings count, access, locked money and the advisor's expected return for each |
| 9 | And what could it become? | Advisor's expected returns over the horizon, with "not a guarantee" on screen |
| 10 | (no question) Note from the advisor | The advisor's own words; the questions return as a list |

Go deeper (offer): a question per bucket ("What does Stability actually do?"),
"What does each product cost?", "What happens in a bad year?" (only with real
data, e.g. each fund's worst fall), "What are SIFs / AIFs?" explainers.

Drop a question when the proposal has no data for it (one option → no
question 8; no advisor expected returns → no question 9).

## Notes from the second test (HyperFrames, 16 Nov 2026)

- Every reveal and highlight follows a voice cue (`{marker}` in the line): the
  bucket colours on their names, product types as the voice lists them, each
  fall as it is read out.
- The three equity-heavy funds had correlations of 0.79 to 0.94, so the mix's
  swing (12.0%) was barely below their weighted average (12.6%). The film says
  so plainly.
- Falls were first mixed across bases (3-year daily for funds); every bar on
  one screen now comes from the same monthly series and period.

# Category film

A briefing on one fund category, usually a general brand version ("A
Growthvine category briefing"). First test: Large & Mid Cap, 41 funds
(16 Nov 2026), 2:33, 13 scenes, MP4 with music, no costs.

## Default question chain (offer it, the advisor edits)

| # | Question | Answered with |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Which of these is worth owning? (cold open) | The number of funds counting up |
| 2 | First, what are these funds, in plain words? | Average company-size split; funds by type (active, index-style, funds of funds); median and largest fund size; how many have a 3-year record |
| 3 | How has a typical fund done? | Average return over 1, 3, 5, 10 years, then lowest, median and highest |
| 4 | Does it matter which fund I pick? | Best against worst fund each period (negatives drawn left of zero); the best-to-worst multiple |
| 5 | What if I invest monthly instead (SIP)? | Lump sum against SIP averages with the SIP range; the market's path that explains the gap |
| 6 | How much do they swing, and how far do they fall? | Lowest-to-highest range bars with the median: swing, worst fall, return for risk; average capture of rises and falls |
| 7 | Does more swing bring more return? | Scatter of every fund with a 3-year record, the top five by Growthvine ranking highlighted |
| 8 | Which funds lead, and are they consistent? | Top funds by Growthvine ranking: stars, 3-year return, quartile over 1, 3, 5 years |
| 9 | What makes the leaders different from each other? | Score parts as a heat grid (no "Cost" part unless costs are in) |
| 10 | Is the biggest fund the best one? | The five biggest funds with their rank |
| 11 | And the newer funds? | How many lack a 3-year record, with their 1-year returns |

Costs (charges, cheapest funds, index-style funds' low cost, cost score) only
if the advisor says yes.

Go deeper (offer): leaders against the category average side by side, the
most concentrated portfolios, comparison with a neighbouring category, how
many funds beat the market (only on one common date for both).

## Notes from the test

- Ratios in words must match the data (42,834 / 2,949 is "about 15 times").
- Fonts may lack symbols (stars): write "5 of 5", never a glyph Poppins
  can't draw.
- Leave room between the longest bar and any big number beside it; scale bars
  to the space, not to a fixed multiplier.
- Long fund names in tight columns: shorten to fit (measure the text) so they
  never overprint the numbers.
- On a scatter, name only the points the text needs; labels inside a dense
  cluster are unreadable, so mention those funds in the side text instead.
